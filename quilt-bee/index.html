Quilt Bee: Block Puzzle

Make a little room for a little joy.

Cozy, untimed block puzzles with satisfying clears and a fresh daily shape. Make a little room, find the perfect fit, and come back tomorrow.

What's inside

No clock, ever

Every round is untimed. Think as long as you like.

A new daily shape

One board a day, with the same pieces for everyone. Finish it to keep your streak growing.

Snug fits

Drop a piece into a gap that fits exactly and earn a bonus.

Helpers when you're stuck

Rotate a piece, undo a move, or swap for a new set.

Clears that sing

Combos answer with soft piano and celesta notes.

Make it yours

Quilt, wood and jewel boards, with palettes and finishes to unlock.
