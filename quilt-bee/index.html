01 / MAKE ROOM

Place. Clear. Keep going.

Fit blocks onto an 8 × 8 board. Complete rows and columns to clear space, build combos, and see how far a little planning can take you.

02 / FIND YOUR SNUG

That just-right feeling.

A piece that fits an empty space exactly earns a “snug” bonus. Leave room for possibilities, then enjoy the moment it all comes together.

03 / A DAILY RITUAL

A new shape to settle into.

Come back for the daily puzzle, with the same board and piece sequence for everyone. Finish a daily board to keep your streak growing.
