Quilt Bee

Request data deletion

Last updated: October 11, 2026

You can ask Ticer Apps to delete the Quilt Bee online identity and leaderboard information we control. You do not need to install or open the game to send a request.

Send your request to ticerapps@gmail.com with the subject “Quilt Bee data deletion request.” This opens your email app; nothing is sent until you send the message.

Please tell us your Game Center or Play Games nickname, whether you play on Apple or Android, and what you want deleted. If your nickname has changed, include the previous one if you know it. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, payment-card numbers, or account credentials.

What happens next

We use the information you provide to find the relevant records. A nickname alone may not identify a player uniquely, so we may ask for reasonable additional information to verify that the records belong to you before changing or deleting them. Please do not send sensitive information unless it is necessary for that verification.

You may request deletion of all Quilt Bee online identity and leaderboard records associated with your verified player identity, or identify particular records you want removed. We will explain the outcome and any information that must be retained, where applicable. We handle requests under applicable requirements; submitting this request does not instantly erase data.

What we can remove

  • The Quilt Bee Firebase user record that connects your platform gaming identity to our online service.
  • Your shared leaderboard entries, including your gaming name, country and platform information, Endless scores, monthly totals, and the Daily scores stored for those totals.
  • Associated user-linked request-limit records and, where applicable, support or purchase-support information processed for us.

We may need to retain limited records for security, fraud prevention, transaction obligations, legal requirements, or to document the request. Provider logs, backups, and independently controlled store records are subject to their own retention and deletion processes; they are not necessarily erased immediately with a leaderboard entry. Our privacy policy explains retention in more detail.

If you connect the game again after deletion, a new online identity can be created and eligible scores still saved on your devices or platform cloud can be submitted again. Let us know if you need help removing those saved sources as part of your request.

Device progress and other providers

Settings → Reset current player data resets the current player’s local progress and this device’s settings and inventory, and attempts to clear the current platform cloud save. It does not delete our Firebase identity or leaderboard records. Another device can retain progress and sync it back. Deleting the app or clearing its storage also does not erase every cloud record.

We cannot delete your Apple Account, Google Account, Game Center or Play Games profile, platform achievements, or store payment records. Apple and Google provide their own account and data controls. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored; resetting progress does not regrant spent coins or helper packs. Purchase providers may retain records needed for ownership, billing, fraud prevention, or legal duties.

For help with any of these steps, email ticerapps@gmail.com. We will help distinguish the records we manage from those a platform provider controls.
