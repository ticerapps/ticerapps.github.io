Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: October 11, 2026

Ticer Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides Quilt Bee. This policy describes information used by the iOS/iPadOS and Android game, including test builds, and these Quilt Bee web pages. Contact us at ticerapps@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

The essentials: your game saves progress on your device. Apple or Google game services can sync supported scores and daily results and record achievements. Our shared leaderboards use Google Firebase and show players from both platforms. Country rankings use optional location permission. Apple or Google and RevenueCat handle in-app purchases; Google AdMob provides ads and consent tools. Online leaderboards use your Game Center or Play Games sign-in; there is no separate Quilt Bee password.

Features and permissions differ by platform, build, connection, and your choices. Test ads and sandbox purchases may still contact their providers. This page describes Quilt Bee specifically; Minesweeper has a separate policy.

1. Your progress and cloud saves

Quilt Bee stores your scores, daily puzzle results, streaks, unfinished rounds, inventory, purchase transaction references, settings, and reminder preferences on your device. It also keeps account-specific progress copies and information needed to associate saves with the correct game-service profile.

Apple Game Center provides your gaming identity and achievements on Apple devices; iCloud stores supported progress under the device’s main iCloud account, with separate save keys for Game Center players. The iCloud Apple Account and Game Center account can be different. Existing guest progress can also use iCloud before Game Center sign-in. Google Play Games Services and Saved Games provide gaming identity, achievements, and signed-in cloud saves on Android.

The platform cloud-save payload contains your best Endless score, best streak, and daily results: each daily score, placement counts, and whether every piece was placed. It does not include the game’s coins, helpers, cosmetic unlocks, settings, or unfinished rounds. Those remain on the device; eligible permanent purchases can separately be restored through the store. Supported progress syncs when the relevant service and account are available; offline changes can remain local until the connection resumes.

Switching game-service accounts changes the associated progress. Separate local copies may remain for previously used accounts. The app uses your player identifier and display name to identify the connected profile and keep progress separate. Account identifiers and account-derived markers are linkable information, even when represented by a hash. Platform cloud saves are separate from the Firebase leaderboard records described below. Apple and Google process cloud information under the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

2. Shared leaderboards and achievements

Quilt Bee’s Global and Country leaderboards combine Apple and Android players. They are operated for Ticer Apps using Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Firestore. Game Center or Play Games sign-in supplies the gaming identity used to create or connect a Firebase user record. You do not enter a separate Quilt Bee password. Players who are not signed in do not submit leaderboard scores.

When connected, the game automatically submits eligible Endless bests and completed Daily scores. Monthly standings total the completed Daily scores for that month. We store your Firebase user identifier, gaming display name, score records, submission/update times, Apple or Android platform, and country code when available. Monthly records also contain daily scores, dates, and the number of days played. Earlier score records may remain from previous versions.

Other signed-in players can see your gaming name, rank, score, platform badge, and monthly days played. Country board membership and the country code in leaderboard results can reveal the country associated with your entry. Results also contain a persistent player key so the app can identify your row. These shared boards are separate from Game Center and Play Games’ own leaderboards; their profile visibility or friends settings do not make a Quilt Bee leaderboard entry private. The game does not request your phone’s address book.

Game Center and Play Games still receive earned achievements and process gaming-service activity under their own policies. Current builds do not post new scores to their native leaderboards; older entries there may remain. See Game Center & Privacy, Google’s Privacy Policy, and Firebase’s privacy information.

Resetting progress or signing out does not remove published scores or the Firebase identity record. You can request deletion of Quilt Bee’s online records. Platform achievements and any older platform leaderboard entries use Apple or Google’s separate controls.

3. Online security and service information

We use Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest or DeviceCheck, or Google Play Integrity, to help check that online requests come from the expected app. These services process app/device integrity information, attestation material, and security tokens. App Check can start when the app launches, including before game-service sign-in. Other enabled SDKs can also contact their providers before you play or purchase.

Firebase and Google Cloud process connection and service information, such as IP addresses, app or device information, request times, authentication activity, and errors, to operate and protect the service. Our leaderboard service keeps user-linked request counters to limit misuse. Its application code does not deliberately write names, scores, request bodies, or authentication tokens to logs; hosting and provider-generated operational or security logs can still exist.

Online leaderboard requests require authentication and app verification and pass through a server that checks submissions and limits requests. Apps cannot directly read or write the leaderboard database. These safeguards reduce risk; they are not a guarantee against all cheating, unauthorized access, or service disruption. See Firebase’s privacy and security information.

4. Optional location for Country rankings

If you enable Country leaderboards, Quilt Bee asks for location access while you use the app. Android requests approximate location only. On iOS, the app requests reduced accuracy and a location estimate sufficient to determine a country; the location returned is controlled by the operating system and your permissions. The game does not request background location access.

The app passes the location estimate to the operating system’s geocoding service to determine a country or territory code. That lookup may contact Apple, Google, or the device’s geocoding provider; it is not guaranteed to stay on the device. Quilt Bee does not save or log the raw coordinates or include them in leaderboard requests. It retains the resulting country code and location preference, and sends the country code with eligible leaderboard records.

The code places your entry on a Country board. Traveling can change the country attached to later submissions; previously submitted records can remain associated with an earlier country. The app does not send these location coordinates to its purchase or advertising integration. Ad services may independently estimate general location from an IP address.

You can decline or revoke location permission and continue playing and using Global rankings. Revoking permission stops new location lookups; it does not erase the country in previously submitted records or automatically clear the stored country preference. Manage permission in your device settings. Apple explains its location services here; Android’s geocoding documentation describes possible network use.

5. Purchases and purchase support

Apple’s App Store or Google Play processes your payment. RevenueCat helps verify transactions, restore purchases, and determine which products you own. This involves purchase receipts or tokens, product and transaction information, purchase history, entitlement status, and an automatically generated customer identifier. Technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and service activity may also be processed.

The game uses RevenueCat’s generated identifier instead of supplying your Game Center or Play Games identity as a customer login. The app does not ask for or send your name, email address, or payment-card details to RevenueCat. The generated identifier can still be linked to purchase records and should not be treated as a promise of anonymous processing.

Purchase information supports validation, fraud prevention, app functionality, and RevenueCat dashboard analytics. We can use purchase/customer records to investigate purchase problems and manage access. The SDK may contact RevenueCat even when you do not complete a purchase. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Deleting game progress does not delete purchase records. For requests concerning purchase-support data processed on our behalf, contact us so we can investigate and, where applicable, instruct our provider. Stores may retain billing records for their own legal and operational purposes. Learn more from the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy, and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising and privacy choices

Quilt Bee uses Google AdMob for between-round and optional rewarded ads, and Google’s User Messaging Platform for applicable consent and privacy choices. Depending on the platform, settings, and ad request, these services and advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and general location estimated from it.
  • Device, app, account, or advertising identifiers, including Android advertising/app set IDs or Apple’s advertising identifier when permitted.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, views, and ads shown.
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information, and information needed to record privacy choices.

This information supports advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Quilt Bee does not pass its cloud-save payload, game-service identity, or raw location coordinates to the ad integration. Applicable consent steps must permit ad requests before the game requests an ad. On iOS, Apple’s tracking permission and Google’s consent choices are separate controls.

Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable. Declining tracking or using test/non-personalized ads does not stop every data transfer. Available privacy messages explain advertising partners and choices. See how Google uses information from apps, the Google Privacy Policy, and Google’s Android and iOS ad-data disclosures.

We do not sell your game-save data or support messages. Advertising activity may be considered sharing or targeted advertising under some laws. You can use the available ad privacy controls described below.

7. Daily reminders

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device using your chosen time and daily completion information. They do not require a Ticer Apps server to send the reminder. Your operating system controls notification permission and delivery. You can change the time, turn reminders off, or try a sample notification in the game’s Settings, and manage notifications in your device settings.

8. Support, diagnostics, and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any details or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to support or privacy requests. Gmail processes those messages as our email provider. Please avoid sending passwords or payment-card details.

Quilt Bee does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics or send a separate stream of gameplay analytics events to a Ticer Apps analytics service. Development builds can log events locally for debugging. This does not exclude the online security records described above or the store, purchase, advertising, and game-service providers’ own analytics and diagnostics.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have added no website analytics, tracking pixels, ads, third-party fonts, or JavaScript. GitHub can process connection information, including IP addresses, as explained in its Privacy Statement. External links lead to services with their own practices.

9. Your choices and deletion requests

  • Ad privacy: use Settings → Privacy choices when the option is available. You can also manage advertising identifiers through Android settings and Apple tracking permission through iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining applicable permissions does not prevent ordinary gameplay.
  • Optional rewarded ads: these are your choice. No Ads or Premium removes between-round ads; optional rewarded ads and some SDK/consent processing can remain.
  • Gaming profiles: manage Game Center or Play Games accounts through the platform’s settings. Signing out does not itself delete cloud progress, the Firebase user record, or previously submitted scores. Platform profile privacy settings do not control the visibility of Quilt Bee’s shared leaderboard entries.
  • Location and reminders: manage the in-game feature settings and the corresponding permissions in your device settings. Location is not needed for ordinary gameplay or Global rankings.
  • Restore purchases: use the game’s Restore purchases option with the store account that made the purchase to restore eligible permanent ownership. A restore does not regrant coin or helper bundles. Purchase access and game progress are handled separately.
  • Reset progress: Settings → Reset current player data resets this player’s local progress and this device’s settings and inventory, and attempts to clear the current platform cloud save. The confirmation explains the reset. It does not delete other players’ local account copies, the Firebase user record, public leaderboard scores, achievements, store/provider purchase records, advertising identifiers, device permissions, or every saved geographic preference. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored; coin and helper bundles are not regranted. Cloud changes require the relevant service to be available, and progress still held by another device can sync back. A reset is not a request to erase every online record.
  • Remove local app storage: use your device’s delete-app or clear-storage controls. Offloading an iOS app retains its data and differs from deleting it. Local removal does not erase cloud saves, store records, or external backups; reconnecting can restore supported cloud progress.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion: see our Quilt Bee data-deletion request page or email ticerapps@gmail.com. We will address information we control or that providers process on our behalf, and help identify controls for data Apple, Google, or other providers hold independently. We may need reasonable information to verify your request. Do not send your password.

10. Retention, security, and your rights

Local progress, account copies, and preferences remain until replaced or removed. Firebase identity and leaderboard records have no automatic expiry in Quilt Bee; they remain until removed through a verified request or service administration. Per-user request counters have a two-day expiry setting; database cleanup is asynchronous. Platform cloud saves and provider-generated logs or security records follow the relevant service’s retention and deletion processes. Support and purchase-related records are kept as needed to provide support, maintain purchased access, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and satisfy applicable obligations. Providers can have separate retention duties, and a reset is not a promise that every backup or provider record is immediately erased.

We use platform storage protections and the security measures of the services involved. This website is served over HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Providers may process information in countries other than yours under their policies and applicable safeguards.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of information; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; opt out of applicable sharing or targeted advertising; or complain to a regulator or appeal a decision. We will handle requests within our control as required by applicable law without penalizing you for exercising your rights.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may rely on providing requested features, consent where required, legitimate interests such as support and service security, or legal obligations. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.
