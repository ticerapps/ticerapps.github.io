Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: October 7, 2026

Ticer Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides Quilt Bee. This policy describes information used by the iOS/iPadOS and Android game, including test builds, and these Quilt Bee web pages. Contact us at ticerapps@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

The essentials: your game saves progress on your device. Connected game services can sync supported scores and daily results, record achievements, and show leaderboards. Location for country or regional rankings is optional. Apple or Google and RevenueCat handle in-app purchases; Google AdMob provides ads and consent tools. No separate Ticer Apps account is required.

Features and permissions differ by platform, build, connection, and your choices. Test ads and sandbox purchases may still contact their providers. This page describes Quilt Bee specifically; Minesweeper has a separate policy.

1. Your progress and cloud saves

Quilt Bee stores your scores, daily puzzle results, streaks, unfinished rounds, inventory, purchase transaction references, settings, and reminder preferences on your device. It also keeps account-specific progress copies and information needed to associate saves with the correct game-service profile.

When connected, Apple Game Center and iCloud support the Apple version’s gaming profile and cloud saves. Google Play Games Services and Saved Games provide these features on Android. The app uses your game-service player identifier and display name to show the connected profile and keep players’ progress separate.

Quilt Bee’s cloud-save payload contains your best Endless score, best streak, and daily results: the daily score, placement counts, and whether every piece was placed. It does not include the game’s coins, helpers, settings, or unfinished rounds. Supported progress syncs when the relevant service and account are available; offline changes can remain local until the connection resumes. iOS and Android use their respective platform services, not a shared Ticer Apps account.

Switching game-service accounts changes the associated progress. Separate local copies may remain for previously used accounts. Account identifiers and account-derived markers are linkable information, even when represented by a hash. We do not run a separate Ticer Apps game-save server. Apple and Google process cloud information under their respective Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

2. Leaderboards, achievements, and sharing

When you are connected to Game Center or Play Games, the game submits eligible scores and earned achievements. It reads leaderboard entries, including player identifiers or display names, scores, and ranks, to show your standing and other players’ results. Friends rankings use information made available by the platform’s gaming service, subject to its permissions and visibility settings. Quilt Bee does not request your phone’s address book.

Your gaming profile name, scores, and achievements may be visible to other players according to the service’s settings. Country boards on iOS and regional boards on Android can also reveal the country or broad region associated with a score. Platform controls affect visibility; resetting local progress does not remove a published leaderboard entry or an achievement. See Game Center & Privacy and Google’s privacy policy.

On supported Apple builds, choosing to share a daily result opens the system share sheet. The result can include the day’s shape, score, puzzle day, and streak. The destination you choose receives that content and handles it under its own policies. Nothing is shared through this feature until you choose to share.

3. Optional location for nearby rankings

If you enable country or regional leaderboards, Quilt Bee asks for location access while you use the app. Android requests approximate location only. On iOS, the app requests reduced accuracy and a location estimate sufficient to determine a country; the location returned is controlled by the operating system and your permissions. The game does not request background location access.

The app passes the location estimate to the operating system’s geocoding service to determine a country or territory code. That lookup may contact Apple, Google, or the device’s geocoding provider; it is not guaranteed to stay on the device. Quilt Bee does not save or log the raw coordinates. It retains the resulting country code, location preference, and the country or region associated with eligible score records.

The code determines which geographic leaderboard receives a new score. Traveling can change the board for new scores; earlier scores can remain on their original boards. The app does not send these location coordinates to its purchase or advertising integration. Ad services may independently estimate general location from an IP address.

You can decline or revoke location permission and continue playing and using available Global or Friends rankings. Revoking permission stops new location lookups; it does not remove previously submitted scores or automatically erase the stored country preference. Manage permission in your device settings. Apple explains its location services here; Android’s geocoding documentation describes possible network use.

4. Purchases and purchase support

Apple’s App Store or Google Play processes your payment. RevenueCat helps verify transactions, restore purchases, and determine which products you own. This involves purchase receipts or tokens, product and transaction information, purchase history, entitlement status, and an automatically generated customer identifier. Technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and service activity may also be processed.

The game uses RevenueCat’s generated identifier instead of supplying your Game Center or Play Games identity as a customer login. The app does not ask for or send your name, email address, or payment-card details to RevenueCat. The generated identifier can still be linked to purchase records and should not be treated as a promise of anonymous processing.

Purchase information supports validation, fraud prevention, app functionality, and RevenueCat dashboard analytics. We can use purchase/customer records to investigate purchase problems and manage access. The SDK may contact RevenueCat even when you do not complete a purchase. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Deleting game progress does not delete purchase records. For requests concerning purchase-support data processed on our behalf, contact us so we can investigate and, where applicable, instruct our provider. Stores may retain billing records for their own legal and operational purposes. Learn more from the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy, and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

Quilt Bee uses Google AdMob for between-round and optional rewarded ads, and Google’s User Messaging Platform for applicable consent and privacy choices. Depending on the platform, settings, and ad request, these services and advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and general location estimated from it.
  • Device, app, account, or advertising identifiers, including Android advertising/app set IDs or Apple’s advertising identifier when permitted.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, views, and ads shown.
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information, and information needed to record privacy choices.

This information supports advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Quilt Bee does not pass its cloud-save payload, game-service identity, or raw location coordinates to the ad integration. Applicable consent steps must permit ad requests before the game requests an ad. On iOS, Apple’s tracking permission and Google’s consent choices are separate controls.

Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable. Declining tracking or using test/non-personalized ads does not stop every data transfer. Available privacy messages explain advertising partners and choices. See how Google uses information from apps, the Google Privacy Policy, and Google’s Android and iOS ad-data disclosures.

We do not sell your game-save data or support messages. Advertising activity may be considered sharing or targeted advertising under some laws. You can use the available ad privacy controls described below.

6. Daily reminders

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device using your chosen time and daily completion information. They do not use a Ticer Apps push-notification server or remote push token. Your operating system controls notification permission and delivery. You can change the time, turn reminders off, or try a sample notification in the game’s Settings, and manage notifications in your device settings.

7. Support, diagnostics, and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any details or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to support or privacy requests. Gmail processes those messages as our email provider. Please avoid sending passwords or payment-card details.

Quilt Bee does not operate a separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting backend. Development builds can log events locally for debugging. Store reporting and the purchase, advertising, and game-service providers’ own analytics and diagnostics are separate from this.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have added no website analytics, tracking pixels, ads, third-party fonts, or JavaScript. GitHub can process connection information, including IP addresses, as explained in its Privacy Statement. External links lead to services with their own practices.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

  • Ad privacy: use Settings → Privacy choices when the option is available. You can also manage advertising identifiers through Android settings and Apple tracking permission through iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining applicable permissions does not prevent ordinary gameplay.
  • Optional rewarded ads: these are your choice. No Ads or Premium removes between-round ads; optional rewarded ads and some SDK/consent processing can remain.
  • Gaming profiles: manage Game Center or Play Games accounts, visibility, and friends permissions through the platform’s settings. Signing out does not itself delete cloud progress or previously submitted scores.
  • Location and reminders: manage the in-game feature settings and the corresponding permissions in your device settings. Location is not needed for ordinary gameplay, Global rankings, or Friends rankings.
  • Restore purchases: use the game’s Restore purchases option with the store account that made the purchase to restore eligible permanent ownership. A restore does not regrant coin or helper bundles. Purchase access and game progress are handled separately.
  • Reset progress: where available, Settings → Reset current player data resets the current player’s local progress and the current account’s game cloud save, along with this device’s settings and inventory. The confirmation explains the reset. It does not delete other players’ local account copies, public leaderboard scores, achievements, store/provider purchase records, advertising identifiers, device permissions, or every saved geographic preference. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored; coin and helper bundles are not regranted. Cloud changes require the relevant service to be available.
  • Remove local app storage: use your device’s delete-app or clear-storage controls. Offloading an iOS app retains its data and differs from deleting it. Local removal does not erase cloud saves, store records, or external backups; reconnecting can restore supported cloud progress.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion: email ticerapps@gmail.com. We will address information we control or that providers process on our behalf, and help identify controls for data Apple, Google, or other providers hold independently. We may need reasonable information to verify your request. Do not send your password.

9. Retention, security, and your rights

Local progress, account copies, and preferences remain until replaced or removed. Cloud records and submitted leaderboard scores remain according to the relevant service’s retention and deletion processes. Support and purchase-related records are kept as needed to provide support, maintain purchased access, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and satisfy applicable obligations. Providers can have separate retention duties, and a reset is not a promise that every backup or provider record is immediately erased.

We use platform storage protections and the security measures of the services involved. This website is served over HTTPS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Providers may process information in countries other than yours under their policies and applicable safeguards.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of information; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; opt out of applicable sharing or targeted advertising; or complain to a regulator or appeal a decision. We will handle requests within our control as required by applicable law without penalizing you for exercising your rights.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may rely on providing requested features, consent where required, legitimate interests such as support and service security, or legal obligations. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.
