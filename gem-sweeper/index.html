Gem Sweeper

Classic Minesweeper. A little more brilliant.

Find the gems. Read the clues.

Choose from four board sizes and four difficulties, with no-guess boards enabled by default. Work through 50 shaped logic puzzles, practice efficient moves in tap drills, or sharpen your pattern recognition in a 60-second Pattern Rush.

Earn cumulative gems, grow your player level and discover 22 jewel ranks. Customize the gem cut, palette, board finish and interface. Optional one-time purchases unlock cosmetics or remove banner and automatic ads; reward videos stay optional.

Help and support

Contact ticerapps@gmail.com. For a bug, include your device model, app version, what happened and the steps to reproduce it. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Replay the first-game guide from Settings → How to play. For missing purchases, sign into the same store account used for the purchase, then choose Restore purchases in Store or Settings. Apple and Google purchases remain within their own store ecosystems.

Game Center/iCloud and Google Play Games support achievements and saved progress on their respective platforms. No separate Ticer Apps account is required. For privacy, records reset, purchase-data questions or deletion requests, see the Gem Sweeper Privacy Policy.
