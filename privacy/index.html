Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy explains how Ticer Apps ("we," "us," or "our"), the independent developer of Minesweeper: Strait of Hormuz (the "game"), handles information in the game and on ticerapps.github.io. Our privacy contact is ticerapps@gmail.com.

At a glance: your progress and custom grids are saved on your device. The game has no Ticer Apps account, cloud-save service, or developer-operated gameplay analytics. Android builds with advertising use Google AdMob and Google's consent tools, which process data separately. We also receive information you choose to send us for support, and GitHub processes connection data when hosting this website.

Current scope: the game is in development, with its Android release planned first and iOS release to follow. Android test builds use test ads, which still connect to Google. Real-money billing, platform cloud saves, and iOS advertising are not currently implemented. This policy does not describe those planned features as active services.

1. Game data on your device

The game saves progress, XP, ranks, scores, statistics, recent match history, puzzle completion, achievements, inventory, settings, unfinished games, and ad/reward limits locally. Custom grids and editor drafts are also stored locally. Local recovery copies help protect against interrupted saves.

We do not upload these saves to a Ticer Apps server. There is currently no account sign-in, online leaderboard, Google Play Games cloud save, Game Center integration, or iCloud synchronization implemented by the game.

If you export a custom grid, the game writes its name and design to a file you select or a local export folder; on some platforms it can copy the grid data to your clipboard. Sharing, moving, or backing up that file through another app is your choice and is subject to that app's practices. Operating-system backups and document access may vary by platform; they are separate from a game-provided cloud-save service.

2. Google advertising and consent

Android builds with advertising integrate Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) for between-match interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy choices. Ad availability depends on your build, connection, region, and applicable privacy settings.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share:

  • Your IP address and an estimate of general location derived from it.
  • Device and account-related identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable identifiers.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, hangs, and energy usage.

Google uses this information for advertising, measurement/analytics, and fraud prevention. UMP also processes information needed to determine applicable privacy messages and record your choices. The game does not provide Google with your game-save file, custom grids, email address, or a Ticer Apps account ID.

Depending on applicable requirements and your choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable. Non-personalized ads and test ads are not necessarily data-free. Consent tools may contact Google to determine whether a message is needed. The game waits for UMP to indicate that ads may be requested before initializing its advertising SDK and loading ads.

Google and applicable advertising partners process information under their own policies and responsibilities. The consent message, where available, identifies relevant partners and choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your support messages or local game saves. Advertising data flows may constitute "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some privacy laws; the controls below explain how to manage available choices.

3. Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, the name shown in your message, and whatever information or attachments you send. We use these to respond, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. Our support mailbox uses Gmail, so Google processes those messages as the email provider. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, forms, or third-party fonts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. Its processing and retention are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

We do not run a separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service. Google advertising diagnostics described above are separate from this. Your operating system or app store may also process information under its own settings and policy.

4. Purchases and planned features

Real-money purchase processing and purchase restoration are not currently implemented. Development builds may display shop prices or test local item unlocks; these are not evidence of a completed store payment. The game does not currently collect payment-card information or send purchase receipts to our own server.

We will revise this policy before introducing real billing, accounts, cloud saves, additional advertising platforms, or other materially different data handling. The current iOS implementation does not include AdMob or an advertising-tracking permission flow.

5. Your choices and controls

  • Ad privacy: use the options in Google's privacy message when it appears. In supported Android builds, Settings → GAME → LEGAL → Privacy Options is shown when Google requires a privacy-options entry point. You can use it to revisit the choices offered for your region. Declining consent does not prevent you from playing the game, although ad availability can change.
  • Device advertising controls: Android provides controls to reset or delete your advertising ID. Their location and available options vary by device and Android version. These controls do not necessarily stop all SDK data processing.
  • Rewarded ads: watching one is optional. A Remove Ads entitlement suppresses between-match interstitials; it does not disable optional rewarded ads or all Google SDK/consent activity.
  • Local data: use your device's app-storage controls to clear app data where available, or uninstall the game. This removes app-local saves, subject to your operating system's behavior. Separately delete exported files, clipboard copies, or backups you have made; clearing app data does not remove copies outside its storage.
  • Support data: contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent us. We cannot retrieve a local save from your device or directly erase records held independently by Google or GitHub, but we can help identify the relevant provider controls.

6. Retention, security, and transfers

Local game data remains until it is replaced or removed from your device. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle your request, follow up on the issue, and meet applicable legal or dispute-resolution obligations. You may request deletion; any necessary exception will be explained. Google and GitHub determine retention for information they process under their respective policies.

Game data uses your device's application storage and its operating-system protections. Google states that data collected by its advertising SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS, and this website is served over HTTPS. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. You control the security of any files you export or share.

Google and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries. Their policies explain applicable transfer safeguards and how to exercise rights concerning their services.

7. Privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; and opt out of applicable sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. You may also have a right to complain to a data-protection authority or appeal a decision about a request. We will respond to requests concerning information within our control as required by applicable law and will not penalize you for exercising applicable rights.

Where European, UK, or Swiss data-protection law applies, we rely on consent where required for advertising-related processing; legitimate interests in responding to support requests and securing our services; and legal obligations where applicable. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Google and GitHub explain their own legal bases in their policies.

To make a request, email ticerapps@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify that a request relates to you. Please do not send identity documents unless we explain why they are necessary.

8. Children's privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact ticerapps@gmail.com so we can investigate and address it, including deleting information within our control where required.

9. Updates to this policy

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. For material changes, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. Please review the policy for the version and features you use.
