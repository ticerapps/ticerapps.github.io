Minesweeper questions

Is Minesweeper luck or logic?

Mostly logic. Every number tells you exactly how many mines touch that tile, and most boards can be cleared by combining those clues. An ordinary random board can still force a 50/50 guess; a no-guess board is generated so that it never does.

What do the numbers in Minesweeper mean?

A number counts the mines in the eight tiles around it, diagonals included. A 1 has exactly one mine among its neighbours. A blank tile (a zero) has none, so the game opens all of its neighbours for you.

What is the 1–2–1 pattern?

Three clues reading 1, 2, 1 along a straight edge of hidden tiles. The mines are under the two 1s and the tile under the 2 is safe. It is one of the most common shapes on any board.

What is no-guess Minesweeper?

A board that a solver has checked so that every safe tile can be reached by deduction from your first move. You can still lose to a mistake, but never to a forced coin flip. Gem Sweeper uses no-guess boards by default, and the Strait of Hormuz browser boards do too.

Is the first click always safe?

In both Ticerapps games, yes, by default. Mines are placed after your first tap, so the tile you choose and the tiles around it start clear. Gem Sweeper lets you switch this off for a harder start.

Can I play Minesweeper free in a browser?

Yes. Minesweeper: Strait of Hormuz has four free boards that run in any browser at minesweeperstraitofhormuz.com, with no download or sign-up.
